For every field the template looks, in order: (1) your extras — any keyword argument you passed to check_value / check_value_in_range; (2) the built-in attribute of the same name; (3) the empty string. So a field you declare but never pass renders as an empty cell, and a field named after a built-in is filled from the built-in unless you also pass it as an extra.
